# Solutions Partner for Data & AI, Infrastructure, and Digital & App Innovation

Feedback

**Appropriate roles**: Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program partner admin

This article describes the _Microsoft Solutions Partner_ program for all three areas of Azure:

-   _Data & AI_
-   _Infrastructure_
-   _Digital & App Innovation_

Each of those three areas has its own, separate Microsoft Solutions Partner designation. To earn the Microsoft Solutions Partner designation for one of those Azure areas, your [partner capability score](partner-capability-score) must meet the qualification requirements for that area. Your scores for _Performance_, _Skilling_, and _Customer success_, which are measured automatically, determine your eligibility.

-   There are common requirements and thresholds for metrics in the _Performance_ and _Customer Success_ categories.
-   There are separate certification requirements in the _Skilling_ category.

To attain a Solutions Partner designation, you must:

-   Earn a minimum of 70 points.
-   Have one or more points for each of the following five metrics:
    -   Performance
    -   Skilling: intermediate certifications
    -   Skilling: advanced certifications
    -   Customer success: usage growth
    -   Customer success: deployments

The following sections describe these requirements and metrics in detail.

Your partner capability score calculates from data already recorded in Partner Center. You can track your progress toward attaining a Solutions Partner for any of the three solution areas by using the information in this article.

## Qualification track classification

Microsoft classifies your organization as either Enterprise or Small and Medium Business (SMB) based on your customer base.

You're classified as SMB if 80% or more of your customers are under the SMB segment. Otherwise, you're classified as Enterprise. The last month's data determines the Customer base calculation.

![Screenshot of a day's snapshot in SMB Enterprise.](media/solutions-partner-azure/smb-enterprise.jpg)

Note

The points a partner earns can differ based on the Qualification Track classification. Performance and Skilling thresholds are different for Enterprise versus SMB.

## Performance

The _Performance_ category measures your organization's ability to increase the number of customers who use Azure services and workloads. The _Net customer adds_ metric is the only metric in the Performance category.

## Net customer adds

**Scoring**:

Each net new customer contributes 10 points, up to a maximum of 30 points.

**Calculation**:

`Net customers added = (Eligible Customers last month) – (Eligible Customers same month last year)`

\*_Eligible Customers_:

**Enterprise**

_Eligible customers_: The number of unique customer tenants contributing at least USD1,000 Azure Consumed Revenue (ACR) in one of the last two months from eligible Azure services associated with eligible offers for specific partner association types.

**SMB**

_Eligible customers_: The number of unique customer tenants contributing at least USD500 Azure Consumed Revenue (ACR) in at least one of the last two months from eligible Azure services associated with eligible offers for specific partner association types.

-   **Eligible workloads**: All Azure Service Level 2 services are eligible workloads. (Workload eligibility is subject to change. Check frequently to ensure that you have the latest workload eligibility information.) For the list of Azure services bundled at Second-highest level, see [Directory of Azure Cloud service](https://azure.microsoft.com/products/). For example, find **Azure Machine Learning** in the section **AI + Machine Learning**.
-   **Eligible offers**: All offers are eligible except _Support_, _Internal_, _Benefit Program_, and _Trial_.
-   **Eligible association types**: CSP (CSP Tier 1 and CSP Tier 2), Digital Partner of Record (DPOR), Partner Admin Link (PAL)

## Skilling

The _skilling_ category uses the number of certified persons in an organization as a measure of partner capabilities and skills.

There are two metrics in this category:

-   [Intermediate certifications](#intermediate-certifications)
-   [Advanced certifications](#advanced-certifications)

The required certifications are different for each of the three Azure areas, as described in the following sections.

### Intermediate certifications

Here are the intermediate certifications requirements.

#### Data & AI

**Enterprise**

**Scoring formula**:

-   Each certified individual contributes up to four points.
-   Your organization can earn up to a maximum of 40 points from 10 individuals who meet the following certification requirements.

**Score calculation**:

To earn points for this metric, follow these steps:

**Step 1: Required certifications**:

-   At least two people must complete the Azure Administrator Associate certification.
-   Completing this step doesn't earn points but it's mandatory before points are awarded in step 3.

**Step 2: Required certifications**:

-   At least two people must complete the Azure Solutions Architect Expert certification.
-   Completing this step doesn't earn points but it's mandatory before points are awarded in step 3.

**Step 3: Other certifications**:

-   Certified individuals (the same or different people as in step 1 or step 2) must complete at least one of the following certifications:

    -   Azure Database Administrator Associate
    -   Azure AI Engineer Associate\*
    -   Azure Data Scientist Associate\*\*
    -   Data Analyst Associate
    -   Azure Cosmos DB Developer Specialty
    -   Microsoft Certified: Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate
    -   Microsoft Certified: Fabric Data Engineer Associate
    -   Microsoft Certified: SQL AI Developer Associate
    -   Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate
    -   Machine Learning Operations Engineer Associate
    -   Azure Databricks Data Engineer Associate

    Each certified individual adds up to four points in step 3.

    \*The Azure AI Engineer Associate certification's retirement date is June 30, 2026. If completed before retirement, it will remain valid for one year.

    \*\*The Azure Data Scientist Associate certification's retirement date is June 1, 2026. If completed before retirement, it will remain valid for one year.


**Important Considerations**:

-   Steps 1 and 2 are mandatory: You must complete step 1 and step 2 before earning any points in step 3.
-   Individuals can be counted across steps. A person can count toward multiple steps, but only once per step.

**Examples**:

Example 1:

-   Step 1: Two people complete the Azure Administrator Associate certification, but you don't earn any points.
-   Step 2: Three people complete step 2, earning 12 points.
-   Step 3: Two people complete Azure Solutions Architect Certification, but you don't earn any points.
    -   Total points = 12.

Example 2:

-   Step 1: Two people complete the Azure Administrator Associate certification, but you don't earn any points.
-   Step 2: Two people, the same two, complete Azure Solutions Architect Certification, but you don't earn any points.
-   Step 3: Two people, the same two, complete step 3, earning eight points.
    -   Total points = 8.

Example 3:

-   Step 1: Two people complete the Azure Administrator Associate certification, but you don't earn any points.
-   Step 2: No individual completes Azure Solutions Architect Certification.
-   Step 3: Two people complete step 3.
    -   Total points = 0 because step 2 wasn't completed.

Example 4:

-   Step 1: One person completes the Azure Administrator Associate certification.
-   Step 2: Two people complete Azure Solutions Architect Certification, but you don't earn any points.
-   Step 3: Two people complete step 3.
    -   Total points = 0 because step 1 wasn't completed. At least two people must complete the certification.

**Important Considerations**:

-   Steps 1 and 2 are mandatory: You must complete step 1 and step 2 before earning any points in step 3.
-   Cap on step 1 total points: No matter how many people complete step 1, you earn a maximum of four points.
-   Cap on step 2 points: No matter how many people complete step 2, you earn a maximum of four points.
-   Individuals can be counted across steps. A person can count toward multiple steps, but only once per step.

**Examples**

Example 1:

-   Step 1: Two people complete the Azure Administrator Associate certification, but you earn four points due to the cap.
-   Step 2: Two people complete Azure Solutions Architect Certification, but you earn four points due to the cap.
-   Step 3: Three people complete step 2, earning 12 points.
    -   Total points = 20.

Example 2:

-   Step 1: Two people complete the Azure Administrator Associate certification, but you earn four points due to the cap.
-   Step 2: Two people, the same two, complete Azure Solutions Architect Certification, but you earn four points due to the cap.
-   Step 3: Two people, the same two, complete step 3, earning eight points.
    -   Total points = 16.

Example 3:

-   Step 1: Two people complete the Azure Administrator Associate certification, but you earn four points due to the cap
-   Step 2: No individual complete Azure Solutions Architect Certification
-   Step 3: Two people complete Step 3
    -   Total points = 4 because only step 1 counts. Step 2 wasn't completed so you get no points for step 3.

Example 4:

-   Step 1: No individual completes the Azure Administrator Associate certification.
-   Step 2: No individual completes Azure Solutions Architect Certification.
-   Step 3: Two people complete step 3.
    -   Total points = 0 because steps 1 and 2 weren't completed.

## Customer success

The _customer success_ category measures your ability to:

-   Increase the use of Microsoft products ([Usage growth](#usage-growth))
-   Expand Microsoft Azure services and workloads utilized by customers ([Deployments](#deployments))

The score calculation for Usage Growth and Deployments metrics is the same for both Enterprise and SMB.

### Usage growth

The _usage growth_ customer success metric measures your ability to increase the use and consumption of qualifying products. You earn points for growth in ACR (Azure consumed revenue) across all of your qualifying customer tenants. Each one percent growth in ACR contributes one point, up to a maximum of 20 points.

**Scoring**:

`Percentage usage growth = (Total qualifying ACR last month - Total qualifying ACR same month last year) * 100 / (Total qualifying ACR same month last year)`

**Qualifying ACR**:

Qualifying ACR is total ACR for the trailing 12 months across the entire customer base from eligible Azure services associated with eligible offers for specific partner association types.

Total ACR must be at least USD1,000 to qualify for scoring.

-   **Eligible Azure services**: All Azure Service Level 2 services are eligible.
-   **Eligible Offers**: All offers are applicable except _Support_, _Internal_, _Benefit Program_, and _Trial_.
-   **Eligible association types**: Eligible attributions for all Azure areas include: CSP Tiers 1 and 2, Digital Partner of Record (DPOR), Partner Admin Link (PAL)

### Deployments

The deployments metric measures the number of advanced Azure services represented in ACR over the last 12 months. An _Azure service_ is defined as a unique, top-level Azure workload indicated by the Service Level 2 data for the deployed tenants. Each eligible deployment contributes two points, up to a maximum of 10 points.

**Eligible deployments**: _Eligible deployments_: The number of unique Azure services across the entire customer base contributing more than zero ACR over last 12 months from eligible Azure services associated with eligible offers for specific partner association types.

-   **Eligible Azure services**: All Azure Service Level 2 services are eligible except Virtual Machines and Virtual Machines Licenses. For the list of Azure services bundled at Second-highest level, see [Directory of Azure Cloud service](https://azure.microsoft.com/products/). For example, find **Azure Machine Learning** in the section **AI + Machine Learning**.
-   **Eligible offers**: All offers are eligible except _Support_, _Internal_, _Benefit Program,_ and _Trial_.
-   **Eligible association types**: CSP (CSP Tiers 1 and 2), Digital Partner of Record (DPOR), Partner Admin Link (PAL)

**Data freshness**:

-   The _Performance_ and _Customer Success_ subcategories typically refresh by the 20th day of every month. But there might be other minor data refreshes throughout the month.
-   _Skilling_ subcategories typically refresh within 10 days after certification completes.

**Enrollment Track**:

The Enrollment track signifies your enrollment into this solution area. The Enrollment track is visible only if you're enrolled in that solution area. Once your organization enrolls in this solution area, the Enrollment Track remains unchanged for one year until the next renewal. The Enrollment track is computed as SMB, if you qualified as SMB at least once during the qualification eligibility window. In the scores history panel, you should have at least one SMB in the six rows. If your organization qualifies as Enterprise all throughout the qualification eligibility window, the Enrollment track computes as Enterprise.

[![Screenshot that shows an example of upcoming enrollment status details.](../media/solutions-partner-azure/upcoming-renewal-status-enroll-track.png)](../media/solutions-partner-azure/upcoming-renewal-status-enroll-track.png#lightbox)

#### Azure China (Mooncake) and Sovereign Cloud subscriptions in Solutions Partner designations and Specializations

Azure subscriptions associated with **isolated or sovereign cloud environments**, such as **Azure China (Mooncake)** and **Fairfax / US Gov‑like clouds**, are **not currently supported** in eligibility and scoring calculations in Solutions Partner designations and Specializations.
